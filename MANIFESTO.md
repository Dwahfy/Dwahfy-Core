# The Dwahfy Manifesto

Dwahfy was born from a single sermon — a Friday khutbah about free speech and the right to speak truth without fear. That idea became a platform.

---

**Speech belongs to people, not platforms.**
No company, algorithm, or government should decide what you are allowed to say.

**The feed is yours.**
What you see is determined by who you follow — not by what we want you to engage with.

**Transparency is not optional.**
Our algorithm is open. You can read it, audit it, question it.

**Your data is yours.**
We don't sell it, we don't hoard it, and you can take it with you.

**Moderation serves the community, not the powerful.**
We remove harm — not dissent.

**This is for everyone.**
Regardless of background, belief, or border, every voice deserves a place to be heard.

---

*Dwahfy exists to give the internet back to the people.*
